🛡️ 1. 100% Local-First Data Storage & Zero Developer Server Telemetry

SlotMind (Ekagra) is engineered under a strict offline-first and zero-knowledge privacy architecture. Your study habits, focus sessions, time logs, notes, and academic records belong entirely to you.

Zero Developer Cloud Databases: Mahendra Dara / Arkyron Studio does not maintain, transmit to, or operate any remote cloud servers or external database infrastructure storing your study sessions, calendar plans, notes, or test scores. (Only standard third-party Google Play Billing and Google AdMob SDKs communicate with Google servers as detailed in Section 5.)
  • Local SQLite Database: All session durations, active study intervals, break times, and segment records are saved strictly on your local device in sandboxed SQLite tables using Android's Room persistence library (Context.MODE_PRIVATE).
  • Local 3-Tier Tasks, Blueprints & 1-4-7 Memory Topics: Your Long-Term, Weekly, and Dated Daily syllabus trees, sub-topic weights, 1-4-7 review schedules, and spaced repetition intervals are stored exclusively on your device.
  • Value Notes Vault, Diagrams & Mock Test Error Vault: Structured notes, custom freehand/shape diagrams, test scores, question accuracy percentages, autopsy reflections, error tags, and local photo/PDF attachment files remain stored solely within your application's private sandbox (/data/data/com.arkyron.slotmind).
  • Offline Integrity Protection (RASP): Runtime application self-protection and signature integrity checks execute 100% locally on your device without transmitting any personal or device data externally.

⚙️ 2. Android Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

SlotMind requests only the exact Android system permissions required to provide accurate, uninterrupted study timers, alarms, and user-initiated file exports. None of these permissions are used for background surveillance or unauthorized data scraping.

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Required to keep the student's active focus stopwatch and 45-10-5 slot countdown running reliably without being prematurely killed by Android's background battery optimizers.
  • WAKE_LOCK & USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Ensures the hardware timer and study alarms wake the CPU on time when the device screen is turned off during study sessions, firing 5m/10m/15m/30m/45m+ break reminders and stage transition cues.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Displays the ongoing foreground study notification and delivers stage transitions, break milestone prompts, and student burnout/wellness thresholds (10h/13h).
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Powers optional Google Play In-App Billing for users choosing to purchase auto-renewing Pro subscriptions (slotmind_pro_sub: monthly, quarterly, biannual, or yearly) or the one-time Lifetime Pro Pass (slotmind_pro_lifetime). All transactions are processed securely by Google Play.
  • INTERNET & AD_ID: Used strictly to validate Google Play purchases and to fetch Google AdMob rewarded video ads on the free tier when the user explicitly taps "Unlock 24-Hour Pass", or to load optional non-intrusive native ads.

💾 4. Complete Data Ownership, User-Initiated Sharing & 1-Tap Wipe

You retain complete, sovereign ownership of your academic records. Because all study data resides locally on your device (android:allowBackup="false"), you have full control over exportarion and deletion at any time:

  • Private Vault Backup & Blueprint Sharing: You can generate an offline JSON backup file from Hub ➔ Cloud Vault, export PDF Note Dossiers, or share Syllabus Blueprint (.json) files with mentors/peers via Android's native FileProvider share sheet. Files leave your device only when you explicitly tap Share or Export.
  • 1-Tap Instant Wipe: You can permanently delete 100% of your sessions, syllabus tasks, 1-4-7 topics, notes, diagrams, and test autopsies at any moment by navigating to Hub ➔ Reset Data (or by uninstalling the application). All local SQLite database tables and sandboxed files are cleared immediately with zero remnants.

🌐 5. Third-Party SDKs, Data Safety & Payment Processing

SlotMind integrates exclusively with official Google Android platform services:

  • Google Play Billing: Subscription (slotmind_pro_sub) and lifetime in-app purchase (slotmind_pro_lifetime) validation is handled directly by Google Play. We never see, collect, store, or process your credit card, debit card, UPI, or banking details.
  • Google AdMob (Free Tier Only): On the free tier, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's Google Advertising ID (GAID/AD_ID), coarse device/network diagnostics, and ad interaction events on Google's servers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Users can reset or delete their Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads. Users who upgrade to SlotMind Pro enjoy a 100% ad-free experience.
