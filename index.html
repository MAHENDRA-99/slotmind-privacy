🛡️ 1. 100% Local-First Data Storage & Zero Developer Server Telemetry

SlotMind is engineered under a strict offline-first, zero-knowledge architecture. Your study habits, focus sessions, notes, test scores, and error logs belong exclusively to you.

  • Zero Developer Cloud Databases: Mahendra Dara / Arkyron Studio does not operate any remote servers, Firebase instances, analytics trackers, or cloud databases storing your study data. Only official Google Play Billing and Google AdMob SDKs communicate with Google servers as disclosed in Section 5.
  • Local Sandboxed SQLite Database: All focus durations, active study intervals, break times, and segment records are stored strictly on your device in private SQLite tables via Android Room (Context.MODE_PRIVATE).
  • Local Tasks, 1-4-7 Memory & Vaults: Your 3-tier tasks, syllabus blueprints, 1-4-7 spaced revision schedules, Value Notes, Sketch Pad diagrams, Mock Test scores, Error Vault entries, and user-selected photo/PDF attachments remain strictly inside the app's private sandbox (/data/data/com.arkyron.slotmind).
  • Offline Integrity Protection: Integrity checks execute 100% locally on your device without transmitting personal data externally.

⚙️ 2. Android Permissions & Strict Exclusions

SlotMind requests only the minimum Android permissions required for accurate background timing, reminders, optional purchases, and optional on-device focus protection:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Keeps your active focus stopwatch and slot timer running reliably when the screen is off.
  • WAKE_LOCK, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & VIBRATE: Fires accurate stage transitions (Focus → Recall → Rest) and break milestone reminders without battery-saver drift.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Displays the live study timer notification, stage cues, and daily wellness check-ins.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS (Optional): Helps prevent OEM battery killers from stopping long study timers.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING, INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly to validate optional Google Play Pro purchases and load non-personalized native ad cards or optional user-initiated rewarded video ads on the free tier.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS & SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Strictly Optional — Focus Distraction Shield Only): Requested only if you manually enable the Focus Distraction Shield (OFF by default; SlotMind remains 100% functional if declined). Used strictly on-device during active focus slots to let you choose installed launcher apps to block, detect when a self-selected distraction app opens, and display a focus reminder card. Installed app lists and app-usage signals are never stored remotely, sold, or shared for advertising or analytics.

Strictly Excluded Permissions: SlotMind NEVER requests Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION), Camera, Microphone, Contacts, External Storage / Gallery permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES), QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES, or BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE. Photo attachments and backups use Android's native Storage Access Framework (SAF) picker so the app accesses only the exact files you explicitly select. The in-app Dopamine Urge Shield is purely a local 10-second mindful breathing prompt.

💾 4. Complete Data Ownership, User-Controlled Backup & 1-Tap Wipe

Because all study data stays strictly on your device (android:allowBackup="false"), you hold complete control over export, auto-sync, and deletion:

  • Private Vault Backup (.zip / .json), Optional Nightly Drive Auto-Sync & PDF/Card Export: You may export an offline .zip Vault archive (containing your local database records and photo/PDF attachments) via Hub ➔ Cloud Vault to local storage or your personal Google Drive via Android SAF, optionally enable nightly on-device auto-sync to your own linked SAF file, export printable A4 PDF dossiers, or share Syllabus Blueprints and branded study cards via Android's native share sheet. Files leave your device only when you explicitly export, share, or link your personal SAF destination.
  • 1-Tap Instant Erasure: You can permanently delete 100% of your sessions, tasks, 1-4-7 topics, notes, diagrams, test logs, and error attachments anytime via Hub ➔ Reset Data (or by uninstalling the app).

🌐 5. Third-Party Services, Non-Personalized Ads & Billing

SlotMind integrates exclusively with official Google Android platform services:

  • Google Play Billing: Optional Pro subscriptions (slotmind_pro_sub) and Lifetime Pro Pass (slotmind_pro_lifetime) are processed solely by Google Play. We never see, collect, or store your card, UPI, or banking details.
  • Google AdMob (Free Tier — Non-Personalized Ads): On the free tier, when ads are shown (such as non-personalized native ad cards or optional user-initiated rewarded video ads), the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares limited technical signals—specifically your device's IP address (which Google may use to estimate general/approximate location), app interactions (such as ad taps and video views), diagnostic information (app/SDK performance metrics), and device/advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, where permitted)—encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention under Google's Privacy Policy. SlotMind never requests Android location permissions and never accesses your GPS or precise physical location.
  • Non-Personalized Ads by Default (npa=1): SlotMind configures AdMob requests with Non-Personalized Ads (npa=1) and a strict Teen/Student content filter (MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_T). We do not run behavioral ad profiling or cross-app interest tracking. SlotMind Pro users experience a 100% ad-free app.
  • Regional Consent (Google UMP): For users in the EEA, UK, and applicable regions, consent preferences are managed via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and can be updated anytime in App Settings.
