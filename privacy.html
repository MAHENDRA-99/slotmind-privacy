🛡️ 1. 100% Local-First Data Storage & Zero Server Telemetry

SlotMind (Ekagra) is engineered under a strict offline-first and zero-knowledge privacy architecture. Your study habits, focus sessions, time logs, and academic records belong entirely to you.

Zero External Server Databases: Arkyron Studio does not maintain, transmit to, or operate any remote cloud servers or external database infrastructure storing your study sessions, calendar plans, or test scores.
  • Local SQLite Database: All session durations, active study intervals, break times, and segment records are saved strictly on your local device in sandboxed SQLite tables using Android's Room persistence library (Context.MODE_PRIVATE).
  • Local Tasks & 1-4-7 Memory Topics: Your multi-tier syllabus trees, sub-topic weights, 1-4-7 review schedules, and spaced repetition intervals are stored exclusively on your device.
  • Mock Test & Error Vault: Test scores, question accuracy percentages, autopsy reflections, error tags, and local photo/PDF attachment URI paths remain stored solely within your application's private sandbox (/data/data/com.arkyron.slotmind).

⚙️ 2. Android Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

SlotMind requests only the exact Android system permissions required to provide accurate, uninterrupted study timers and notifications. None of these permissions are used for data scraping, background surveillance, or ad-profiling.

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Required to keep the student's active focus stopwatch and 45-10-5 slot countdown running reliably without being prematurely killed by Android's aggressive background battery optimizers.
  • WAKE_LOCK & USE_EXACT_ALARM: Ensures the hardware timer and study alarms wake the CPU on time when the device screen is turned off during study sessions, firing 15m/30m break reminders and slot transition cues.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Displays the ongoing foreground study notification and delivers break milestone prompts (15m/30m) and student burnout/wellness thresholds (10h/13h).
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Powers optional Google Play In-App Billing for users choosing to purchase the lifetime VIP pass (slotmind_pro_lifetime). Transactions are processed securely by Google Play.
  • INTERNET & AD_ID: Used strictly to fetch Google AdMob rewarded video ads on the free tier when the user explicitly taps "Unlock 24-Hour Pass", and to load optional non-intrusive native ads.

💾 4. Complete Data Ownership, Export & 1-Tap Wipe

You retain complete, sovereign ownership of your academic records. You can backup, transfer, or permanently delete your data at any time:

  • Private JSON Vault Backup: You can generate an encrypted, offline JSON backup file of your entire database directly from Hub ➔ Cloud Vault and store it on your own device, SD card, or private Google Drive folder.
  • 1-Tap Instant Wipe: You can permanently delete 100% of your sessions, syllabus tasks, 1-4-7 topics, and test autopsies at any moment by navigating to Hub ➔ Reset Data. All SQLite database tables are cleared immediately with zero remnants.
